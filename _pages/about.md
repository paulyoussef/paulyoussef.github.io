Hi! My name is Paul. I'm a PhD Student at Marburg University in Germany. I'm supervised by [Prof. Dr. Christin Seifert](https://aix-group.github.io/cseifert/).
My research focuses on diverse facets of factual knowledge in large language models like probing models for factual knowledge, editing factual knowledge, identifying edited models, and countering malicious editing. I'm also interested in interpretability.
